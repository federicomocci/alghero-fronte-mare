Beaches

The best beaches near Alghero. From the famous to the hidden, with honest advice on what to expect.

🏖️ Nearby beaches (10-15 min)

These beaches are close to Alghero and easy to reach. Perfect for a half-day trip.

Le Bombarde

⭐ Most popular 10 min by car

The most famous beach near Alghero. White sand, crystal clear water, pine forest behind. Gets very crowded in August — arrive early or go in June/September.

💡 Tip Parking fills up by 10am in high season. There's a beach bar for lunch and drinks.
📍 View on Google Maps

Lazzaretto

Less crowded 10 min by car

Next to Le Bombarde but smaller and usually less crowded. Rocky areas good for snorkeling. More intimate atmosphere.

📍 View on Google Maps

Mugoni

Family-friendly 15 min by car

Long sandy beach with shallow water — perfect for families with children. Inside Porto Conte natural park. Good facilities, easy parking.

📍 View on Google Maps

🚗 Worth the drive (30-45 min)

These require more effort but offer something special.

Porto Ferro

Wild beach 30 min by car

Large, wild beach facing west. Dramatic scenery, often waves — popular with surfers. No facilities, bring everything you need. Spectacular sunsets.

💡 Tip The last stretch of road is unpaved. Go for sunset with a picnic.
📍 View on Google Maps

Argentiera

Unique atmosphere 45 min by car

Former mining village with a beach. Haunting industrial ruins meet turquoise water. Not the most beautiful beach, but unforgettable atmosphere.

📍 View on Google Maps

La Pelosa

⭐ Most beautiful 45 min by car

One of the most famous beaches in the Mediterranean. Caribbean-like water, iconic tower. Extremely crowded in summer — booking and entrance fee required.

💡 Tip In summer you must book online and pay €3.50 entrance. Arrive very early. Better in June or September.
📍 View on Google Maps

💡 Beach tips

  • Best months: June and September — warm water, fewer crowds
  • Avoid: August weekends at popular beaches
  • Bring: Water shoes for rocky entries, snorkel gear
  • Umbrella rental: €15-25/day at equipped beaches
